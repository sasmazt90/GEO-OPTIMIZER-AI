GEO Optimizer AI: measure and improve your visibility in AI answers

GEO Optimizer AI is an SEO and generative engine optimization (GEO) platform. It measures how often ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity name your brand for the questions your buyers ask, audits whether search and AI crawlers can read your site, and helps you create content that answer engines can extract and cite.

What can you do with GEO Optimizer AI?

  • Visibility Tracking: track a set of buyer questions per brand and market. Every question is asked live on each AI model several times; brand mentions, position, share of voice and cited sources are counted and charted over time.
  • Site Audit: a reproducible, rule-based audit of crawl access, indexability, on-page SEO, structured data and content extractability, with a fix list ordered by impact.
  • AI Crawler Access: robots.txt rules and live HTTP status for 14 crawlers, including OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, GPTBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot, Googlebot and Bingbot.
  • Benchmark: see which brands live AI answers name for any question, counted from real answers.
  • Brand Entity, Query Intelligence and Prompt Research: check entity signals, expand a query into the sub-questions AI systems explore, and build a prompt set to monitor.
  • Content Studio: check existing pages against GEO rules and draft answer-first landing pages and content briefs.

How is AI visibility measured?

Each tracked question is sent to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity models through their APIs, one to five times per model. The platform then counts how many answers name your brand, where it appears among the brands in each answer, and which websites the answer cites. Visibility is reported as the share of answers that mention the brand, with a 95% confidence interval. Nothing is estimated by a model.

What does the site audit check?

More than 30 checks on the raw HTML a crawler receives: HTTP status and redirects, robots.txt rules per crawler, XML sitemap, llms.txt, noindex and canonical tags, title and meta description, heading structure, image alt text, JSON-LD validity, Organization and page-type schema, text available without JavaScript, answer-first openings, question-style headings and freshness signals.
